# Privacy Policy for GrabTheCubeDropTheCube

Last updated: 10 October 2026

GrabTheCubeDropTheCube ("the app") is published by Stuff By Ellis.

## Data we collect

None. The app does not collect, store, transmit or share any personal
information or device information. It has no accounts, no analytics, no
advertising and no network features.

## Hand and head tracking

The app uses the headset's hand and head tracking so you can interact with
objects. This data is processed on your device while the app is running and
is never recorded or sent anywhere by the app.

## Meta platform

The app is distributed through the Meta Horizon Store. Meta may collect data
about your use of its platform under its own privacy policy, which is
separate from this one.

## Deleting your data

Because the app collects no data, there is nothing for us to delete.
